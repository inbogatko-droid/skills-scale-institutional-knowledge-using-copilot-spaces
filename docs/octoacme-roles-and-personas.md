# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Key Interactions
- **With Tech Lead/Architect**: Receives design guidance, participates in technical design reviews
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborates on testability and edge cases, addresses defects
- **With Project Manager**: Provides status updates and impediment escalation

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Aligns on roadmap, timeline, and resource planning
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Validates business requirements and priorities
- **With Tech Lead/Architect**: Discusses technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Defines acceptance criteria and quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager**: Aligns on priorities and backlog planning
- **With Developers**: Gathers status, manages impediments and blockers
- **With Tech Lead/Architect**: Escalates technical risks and dependencies
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Tracks quality gates and release readiness
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Provides status updates and escalations
- **With Business Analyst**: Coordinates requirements gathering and documentation

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, acceptance testing, and quality metrics for a project. They ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans and test cases aligned with acceptance criteria
- Establish quality gates and acceptance criteria verification procedures
- Identify and document defects and regressions with severity and priority
- Collaborate with developers on testability, edge cases, and test coverage
- Report quality metrics and trends to PM and stakeholders
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate release readiness and coordinate smoke tests

### Goals
- Ensure zero critical defects in production
- Reduce test cycle time through automation
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Establish clear quality standards and metrics

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Reviews acceptance criteria, provides test feedback, identifies edge cases, participates in design reviews for testability
- **With Product Manager**: Validates acceptance criteria, communicates quality risks and trade-offs
- **With Project Manager**: Reports test readiness status, flags quality blockers, updates release readiness checklist
- **With Tech Lead/Architect**: Discusses testing strategy and technical approaches for quality assurance

### Typical Communication
- QA sign-off in Definition of Done
- Test status updates in standups and weekly syncs
- Defect triage and quality metrics reports
- Pre-release quality verification and smoke test coordination

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads or Architects provide technical direction, design guidance, and architectural oversight. They ensure technical decisions align with long-term strategy and mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical architecture and design decisions for scalability and maintainability
- Review technical designs and PRs for quality, alignment with standards, and risks
- Identify and escalate technical risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Mentor developers and support skill development and knowledge sharing
- Ensure code quality and maintainability standards are met
- Recommend tools, frameworks, and technical approaches
- Collaborate on testability and design for testability with QA

### Goals
- Maintain technical excellence and code quality
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable scalability and long-term maintainability
- De-risk technical decisions and dependencies

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provides design guidance, reviews technical decisions, mentors on best practices, conducts architecture reviews
- **With Project Manager**: Flags technical risks and dependencies early, escalates blockers, provides feasibility assessments
- **With Product Manager**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs, discusses long-term architectural implications
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Advises on testability strategy and quality assurance approach

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- PR code review comments and guidance
- Technical risk register contributions
- Architectural decision documentation and rationale

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders or Sponsors are business and decision-making leaders who define business requirements, approve scope, and champion the project. They provide business context and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success outcomes for the project
- Approve scope, budget, and resource allocation
- Champion the project and maintain executive visibility
- Provide feedback on solutions and validate business value
- Escalate organizational blockers and remove barriers
- Communicate project vision and benefits to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers expected business value and ROI
- Align project with organizational strategy and priorities
- Maintain stakeholder engagement and executive support
- Enable swift decision-making and issue resolution

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager**: Aligns on business requirements, priorities, and success metrics
- **With Project Manager**: Receives status updates, approves major decisions, provides escalation support
- **With Developers and Tech Lead**: Understands technical approaches and major design decisions
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Informed of quality status and release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and business reviews
- Milestone approvals and go/no-go decisions
- Executive briefings on project status and risks
- Business value and impact communication

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies and coaching on the team's agile practices. They remove impediments, coach the team on agile principles, and help continuously improve delivery processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers that slow down the team
- Coach team members on agile practices and principles
- Track sprint velocity and burndown, identify trends
- Support process improvements based on retrospective feedback
- Educate team on agile ceremonies, roles, and responsibilities
- Help navigate team dynamics and conflict resolution

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery efficiency
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Enable self-organizing teams that make autonomous decisions
- Remove obstacles to team productivity

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Supports project planning and status communication, escalates organizational blockers
- **With Developers**: Coaches on agile practices, facilitates ceremonies, removes technical blockers
- **With Product Manager**: Helps refine backlog, manages scope and priority discussions
- **With All Roles**: Facilitates cross-functional collaboration and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Process improvement tracking and action items
- Velocity and burndown metrics reporting
- One-on-one coaching and team development conversations

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, document, and validate business requirements. They bridge business and technical teams by translating business needs into clear acceptance criteria and technical specifications.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather business requirements
- Document user stories, use cases, and acceptance criteria
- Validate requirements completeness and testability
- Identify gaps and assumptions that need clarification
- Support requirements traceability throughout project lifecycle
- Collaborate with technical team on feasibility and design implications
- Participate in reviews to ensure solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Reduce misunderstandings and rework due to unclear requirements
- Enable smooth communication between business and technical teams
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business objectives

### Key Interactions
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Gathers business requirements and validates priority
- **With Product Manager**: Refines backlog items and acceptance criteria
- **With Developers and Tech Lead**: Explains business context and validates design feasibility
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Develops test cases and acceptance criteria verification
- **With Project Manager**: Updates requirements documentation and tracks traceability

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Backlog refinement sessions and story clarifications
- Requirements validation reviews and walkthroughs
- User story and acceptance criteria collaboration

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps engineers or Release Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release processes. They enable reliable, automated, and repeatable deployments to production.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated testing and deployment
- Manage infrastructure and environment configuration (dev, staging, production)
- Automate build, test, and deployment processes
- Monitor application performance and infrastructure health post-deployment
- Manage release schedules and coordinate deployment activities
- Document runbooks and incident response procedures
- Support rollback and incident management processes
- Ensure security scanning and compliance in deployment pipeline

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments to production
- Reduce deployment risk and manual effort
- Minimize downtime and enable quick issue resolution
- Maintain infrastructure stability and performance

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Supports deployment troubleshooting, receives code changes for pipeline integration
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinates smoke tests and post-deployment verification
- **With Project Manager**: Provides deployment status and release readiness assessment
- **With Tech Lead/Architect**: Advises on infrastructure and deployment strategy decisions

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment runbooks
- CI/CD pipeline status and build notifications
- Post-deployment health checks and monitoring reports
- Incident response and rollback coordination
