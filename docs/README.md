# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview
OctoAcme runs projects with a customer-first, iterative delivery mindset. Projects follow a clear lifecycle of initiation, planning, execution, release, and close/retrospective, governed by principles like clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Teams produce and maintain core artifacts — Project Charter, roadmap and release plan, sprint/iteration backlog, acceptance criteria/Definition of Done, risk register, and retrospective notes — to keep scope, success metrics, and risks visible.

## Key workflows
Initiation captures problem statements, stakeholders, and high-level timelines; planning defines scope, resources, milestones, and dependencies; execution mixes build, test, review, and iteration; release covers deploy/verify/announce; and close captures learnings and next steps. Small, testable increments, acceptance criteria and a formal Definition of Done keep delivery safe and predictable.

## Roles & communication
Named Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize the backlog; Developers implement features and tests; QA validates acceptance; and Stakeholders provide inputs and approvals. Communication cadence is explicit — weekly PM–PdM syncs, twice-weekly delivery standups (or as agreed), and monthly stakeholder updates — supplemented by PR descriptions, design docs, risk registers and decision logs to keep alignment and traceability.

## Quality & continuous improvement
Developers maintain tests, documentation and observability; QA validates acceptance criteria and signs off releases; retrospectives capture action items and feed continuous improvement. Risk registers and decision logs are kept current to reduce future escalations and speed future delivery.
